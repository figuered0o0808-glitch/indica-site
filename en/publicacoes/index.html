Paper · 2026

Price formation in the influence market

Francisco Figueiredo · 36 pages

Why the price of sponsored content lies far above its production cost and varies so much across similar creators. Price pays for access to a reputational stock — credibility, affect and legitimacy — and is bargained between a floor, set by the creator's costs and risk, and a ceiling, set by what the advertiser can and wants to pay.
