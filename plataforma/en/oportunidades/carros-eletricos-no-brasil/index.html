Overview

INDICA announces a public call to receive and select proposals for digital communication solutions. Content creators, digital influencers, communication agencies, creative or media collectives and independent producers may participate.

The aim is to increase public awareness of the transition to sustainable mobility in Brazil by broadening access to information on the purchase and maintenance costs of clean vehicles, the environmental and social benefits of electrification and public policies such as the Mover Program.

The focus is the digital ecosystem: social networks, video and audio platforms and online media. In-person activities or street interventions are also permitted and encouraged, provided they are integrated into the digital distribution and amplification strategy.

Proposals must include an action to promote the Eletrifica Já campaign. INDICA will provide the materials and specify the channels to be promoted during alignment with the selected applicants.

Project formats

Video, audio, text and in-person activities connected to digital distribution. A proposal may combine several formats.

01

Short videos

Reels, TikToks and other short videos for science communication and education about electric vehicles.

02

YouTube productions

Long-form videos exploring costs, public policy, sustainability and electrification.

03

Podcasts and audio

Podcasts and other audio content, according to the proposed communication project.

04

Carousels, articles and threads

Educational carousels, articles and threads explaining the data, concepts and information in the brief.

05

In person + digital

Street interventions, public activities and other in-person actions that generate content and engagement on digital channels.

06

Multiplatform projects

Combinations of video, audio, text and in-person activities with an integrated digital distribution strategy.

These examples appear in item 3 of Annex I of the call document. In-person activities are optional and must generate content and engagement on digital platforms. All proposals must follow the guiding brief and the rules of the call.

What is supported

  • Individuals, including content creators and digital influencers, and legal entities, including agencies, production companies and media collectives, that are Brazilian or resident in Brazil.
  • The document includes MEI, EIRELI and limited liability companies among the eligible legal entities.
  • Technical and financial proposals for scientific, educational and public awareness communication about electric vehicles in Brazil.
  • Digital solutions in video, audio, text, educational carousels and other formats; in-person activities are permitted when integrated into digital channels.
  • The themes in the guiding brief are suggestions, not mandatory requirements: costs and accessibility; public policies and the Mover Program; sustainability and energy transition; myths and facts about electrification. Other approaches related to electric mobility in Brazil and the objectives of the call are also welcome.
  • Proposals must include an action to promote the Eletrifica Já campaign. INDICA will provide the materials and specify the channels to be promoted during alignment with the selected applicants.

Selection criteria

Each proposal will receive an overall score from 0 to 10. Selection does not necessarily follow score order: it also considers the available budget, requested amounts, feasibility and the combination of projects supported. The organization may publish only the selected proposals, without scores or ranking, or include scores and/or ranking in the public results.

01

Overall proposal score

An overall assessment of the proposed communication solution, with a single score from 0 to 10 and no separate component scores.

Budget rules

  • The total budget is up to R$ 100,000.00. The organization is not required to invest the full amount; funding depends on the number, feasibility and relevance of the approved proposals.
  • Each project may request up to R$ 30,000.00. The amount requested must reflect the scope, planned deliverables and justified costs, without compromising quality or delivery capacity.
  • Between 5 and 15 proposals will be selected, depending on the applicants’ profiles, the amounts proposed and the total budget. There are no fixed selection quotas by funding range.
  • At most two proposals with an individual amount above R$ 25,000.00 will be selected, subject to the R$ 30,000.00 cap per project. The preference is to fund only one proposal in this range.
  • Lower-budget proposals will have a greater chance of selection when the budget is compatible with the scope, quality of deliverables and delivery capacity. This priority seeks to broaden the diversity of projects and communication channels supported.
  • The organization may propose changes to scope and budget adjustments as a condition for signing the contract, without being required to approve the full amount requested.

Supporting documentation (optional)

  • Files and links are optional. Applicants may submit a proposal without attachments or portfolio links; the materials below can supplement the information provided in the form.
  • Official identification: RG or CNH for individuals; proof of CNPJ registration and current articles of association or bylaws for legal entities.
  • Presentation of the applicant, agency or collective, with a brief history, institutional portfolio or professional profiles of those involved.
  • Up-to-date links to profiles, active channels and/or a portfolio of previously produced digital content.
  • An updated media kit or official platform analytics reports, with reach, engagement and demographic profiles fully open to verification by the committee.
  • Technical and financial proposal: an optional file covering formats, distribution plan, editorial approach, schedule and, where applicable, an itemized budget spreadsheet. In-person activities must explain their integration into the digital ecosystem.

Obligations and commitments of selected applicants

  • Deliver and publish the solution in accordance with the approved proposal and the guiding brief in Annex I.
  • Submit scripts, artwork, street activity plans and content previews to INDICA for technical validation before publication.
  • Keep metrics for reach, impressions, full views, engagement, saves and retention open to real-time monitoring and auditing by INDICA.
  • Keep digital publications active and publicly accessible for at least one year after publication.
  • Deliver the consolidated results report by the deadline set in the schedule.
  • Clearly identify sponsored educational content or the partnership, in accordance with social networks’ regulatory guidelines.
  • Deliver the action to promote the Eletrifica Já campaign described in the approved proposal, using the materials and channels specified by INDICA during alignment with the selected applicants.

Annex I — guiding brief

Communication must consider the economic and structural challenges of electric mobility in Brazil, including purchase costs and the infrastructure required for consumer adoption.

The themes below are suggestions to help develop proposals. Applicants are not required to follow any of them; other approaches are welcome, provided they relate to electric mobility in Brazil and the objectives of the call.

Proposals may combine short videos, Reels or TikTok, long-form YouTube videos, podcasts, educational carousels, articles and threads. In-person activities are optional and must generate content and engagement for digital platforms.

Proposals must include an action to promote the Eletrifica Já campaign. INDICA will provide the materials and specify the channels to be promoted during alignment with the selected applicants.

INDICA will provide reference materials and information about the channels to be promoted during alignment with the selected applicants.

  • Theme A — Costs and accessibility: purchase, maintenance, refueling or recharging and depreciation compared with combustion-engine vehicles.
  • Theme B — Public policies and the Mover Program: guidelines, incentives, domestic production and decarbonization.
  • Theme C — Sustainability and energy transition: reducing greenhouse gas emissions and improving air quality in metropolitan areas.
  • Theme D — Myths and facts about electrification: battery range, charging time and disposal of components.
  • An informative, evidence-based tone, with official data and accessible language; a constructive approach that encourages critical reflection and informed debate.
  • Accessibility and inclusion: captions, clear audio and educational visual resources.

Campaign guidance

INDICA will provide reference materials and communication channels during the alignment with selected applicants.

General provisions
